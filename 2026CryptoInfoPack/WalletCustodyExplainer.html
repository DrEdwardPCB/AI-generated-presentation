Your Crypto, Simplified

A familiar banking experience without managing wallets or keys

The Common Assumption

Many people believe using crypto means every customer must own their own wallet, manage private keys, and directly interact with the blockchain.

Traditional View

One customer → One wallet → One set of keys

Modern Financial Institution Model

Many customers → Bank‑managed wallets → Secure internal tracking

What Happens Behind the Scenes

Bank Wallets

The bank maintains highly secure crypto wallets designed for safety, liquidity, and operational efficiency.

Virtual Allocation

Your crypto balance is recorded internally, similar to how banks track deposits today.

Transactions

When you send crypto, the bank executes the blockchain transaction on your behalf.
