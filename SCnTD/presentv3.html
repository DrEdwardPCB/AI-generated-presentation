Key Terms — Simple & Layered

Understand the Stack Before the Strategy

Layer 1: Infrastructure
⛓️
Blockchain — a shared ledger that tracks balances & transfers. Think of it as the “railway tracks.”

Examples: Ethereum, permissioned chains

Layer 2: Protocol
⚙️
Smart Contracts — code that runs on the blockchain to enforce rules automatically. “The signals & switches.”

Token standards (e.g., ERC-20) live here

Layer 3: Application
💲
Stablecoins — digital tokens pegged to fiat. Public-chain instruments; price-stable but outside deposit insurance.
🏦
Tokenized Deposits — bank-issued, 1:1 on-chain representations of deposits, within regulatory perimeter; integrates with bank systems.

End-user value: payments, treasury, settlement

In short: L1 = the tracks, L2 = the rules, L3 = the services customers feel. Our strategy choices live at Layer 3.

Opportunities & Challenges

Stablecoins vs Tokenized Deposits — Our View

Positioning: Stablecoins are primarily a central bank/sovereign domain or to be used via highly regulated partners. Scotiabank’s focus: Tokenized Deposits to enhance liquidity and keep funds within the bank.
Stablecoins (Layer 3 — public-chain token)
✓
Opportunities: 24/7 transfers; global reach; programmable.
✓
Low base network fees; potential for new fee models.
!
Challenges: No deposit insurance; reserve/peg risks; evolving regulation.
!
Off-platform leakage (funds moving to external wallets/exchanges).
Tokenized Deposits (Layer 3 — bank-issued)
✓
Opportunities: Inside bank perimeter; deposit insurance eligibility (jurisdiction-dependent).
✓
24/7 “on-us” wallet-to-wallet settlement; programmable controls.
✓
Enhances liquidity: sweeps, pooling, intercompany transfers.
!
Challenges: Integration & interop; custody & key management operations.

Traditional Deposit Use Case (Intra-company transfer)

1
Move request hits core banking; queued for next window.
2
Cut-offs/cycle times; reconciliation next day.
3
T+0–1 posting; limited programmability.

Tokenized Deposit Use Case (Intra-company transfer)

1
Instant wallet-to-wallet “on-us” move (24/7).
2
Smart-contract controls (limits, allow-lists, approvals).
3
Immediate finality + real-time visibility for treasury.

Result: faster liquidity, fewer fails, and better deposit retention “on-us.”

Conclusion & Next Steps

Adopt? YES — With Caution & a Tokenized-Deposit First Strategy

Strategic Emphasis

Prioritize Tokenized Deposits to enhance liquidity products, create “on-us” 24/7 mobility, and retain deposits within Scotiabank. Use stablecoin rails only via tightly controlled, regulated partners/corridors where they demonstrably add value.

Phase 1 (Q4 2025)

  • Institutional pilot: wallet-to-wallet tokenized deposits for top corporates.
  • KPIs: settlement time, fail rate, NPS, deposit retention, fee margin.
  • Risk controls: allow-lists, limits, automated monitoring.

Phase 2 (2026)

  • Expand corridors (Canada ↔ LATAM) & ERP integrations.
  • Selective interop with bank networks; compliant public-chain access.
  • Liquidity products: real-time sweeps, pooling, programmable escrow.

Phase 3 (2027)

  • Retail integration: instant P2P, in-app remittances with guarantees.
  • Scale deposit lift & cross-sell; enterprise-grade ops & resilience.

Executive Takeaway

  • Yes to adoption — start with tokenized deposits where we can control risk and maximize client value.
  • Goal: Liquidity enhancement & deposit retention; faster, cheaper, programmable value transfer “on-us.”
  • Guardrails: Regulatory alignment, strong key management, real-time monitoring, staged interop.
