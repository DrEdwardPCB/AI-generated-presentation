What is Core Banking?

Core banking is the heart of the bank — the system that manages all products, customer accounts, and daily transactions.

🏦 Account Management

Houses and maintains customer savings, checking, and loan accounts.

💸 Transaction Processing

Processes deposits, withdrawals, transfers, and payments in real time.

📈 Interest & Fees

Automatically calculates interest, fees, and charges accurately.

📑 Compliance & Reporting

Ensures regulatory compliance and generates internal/external reports.

🔗 Integration

Connects with ATMs, mobile, and online banking channels.

🧾 Pricing & Billing

Supports pricing analytics, charge posting, and billing management.

What’s More for a Modern Core Banking System

The modern core banking system extends beyond traditional capabilities to provide real-time intelligence, flexibility, and end-to-end visibility.

⚡ Real-Time Operations

Posting and analysis are fully streamlined — enabling instant insight and faster decision-making.

⚙️ Completely Configurable Products

Reduces dependency on multiple systems by allowing fully configurable products that adapt quickly to market needs.

📊 Sophisticated Reporting

Every event is logged, supporting detailed reports and full audit trails for compliance and transparency.

💬 Rich Transaction Data

Captures more context — distinguishing between pre-authorized payments and post-billed items (e.g., Enbridge vs. Rogers).
